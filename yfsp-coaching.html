Questions

I have zero experience. Is that okay?

Yes. This is made for beginners. We start with the basics and go one step at a time.

Do I need to be a great illustrator?

No. Some students draw, some use Canva, some hire a designer. I'll help you find the way that works for you.

I work full time. Do I have time for this?

Yes. YFSP is made for people with a 9-5. Each step is small, so you can work on evenings and weekends. Plan on about [3 to 5] hours a week, plus our calls.

I live outside the US. Can I join?

Yes. Everything happens online, and the steps work wherever you are.
